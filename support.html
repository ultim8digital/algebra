ALGEBRA HANDBOOK

How can we help?

Find an article

On the Handbook tab, search by the name of a rule, a formula or a keyword. Pick a topic with the chips above the list or show only your starred articles.

Read a proof

Rules and theorems come with a proof or a derivation. “When it applies” and “Common mistakes” show where a rule stops working, and every article has worked examples.

Live graphs

Open the Graphs tab or the graph inside an article and move the sliders: the curve and its key points update as you go.

Step-by-step solver

Open the Solver tab, choose the type of problem and enter your numbers. The app shows every step of the solution and the answer. If a problem has no solution, it says so and explains why.

Practice

Open the Practice tab and choose a topic. Problems are generated anew every time; after each answer you see an explanation. Stars and the number of solved problems are kept on your device and can be reset in About.

Visual proofs

The Visual tab shows proofs in pictures. Use the arrows to go step by step.

Cheat sheet and tables

The cheat sheet lists formulas without explanations, for all topics or only for your favorites. The reference screens hold 20 tables, the notation and a guide to solving problems.

Change the language

Open About and choose the language. English, Spanish, Arabic, French, Hindi, Thai and Russian are available offline. Formulas follow each language’s school notation.

Report a problem or a correction

Email ultim8digital@gmail.com with the app version (shown in About), your device and operating-system version, and what you did before the problem appeared. For a mathematical or translation correction, name the article and the exact line. Please do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

About the content

The handbook follows standard school treatments of algebra and introductory calculus. It is a study aid and a quick reference, not an official textbook of any school system. Corrections are welcome.
