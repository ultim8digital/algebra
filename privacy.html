ALGEBRA HANDBOOK

Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Algebra Handbook is an offline educational algebra reference published by Oleg Korunov. This policy describes the mobile app distributed through Google Play and the App Store. The same policy is available inside the app, under About → Privacy.

No accounts, no tracking

The app does not create accounts and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code. It does not send us personal information and does not contact our servers. The Android release does not request internet access at all.

What is stored on your device

Your language, theme, favorites, practice results, Pro entitlement (including subscription expiration or Lifetime status), and update-notice preferences are saved locally. Solver inputs, searches, AI questions and scanned images are not sent to our servers. Tutor conversations stay in memory and are discarded when the conversation screen closes.

The handbook works offline

The articles, graphs, solvers, translations and fonts are installed with the app. These original learning tools work without a network connection. The optional iOS AI tutor uses Apple Intelligence on a compatible device; the system model or an offline language pack may need an initial download.

Device permissions

The iOS camera opens only when you request a problem scan. Vision recognizes text on the device, and the app does not save the captured image. You review and may edit the recognized text before asking the tutor. The system photo picker shares only the selected image, without full-library access. Microphone, contacts and location are not used.

On-device AI and translation

The optional algebra tutor uses Apple Foundation Models on the device. When a question language is not directly supported, Apple's on-device Translation may be used; a language download or English fallback may be required. Questions, photos and generated answers are not uploaded to a developer server. AI and OCR can make mistakes; review scanned symbols and verify calculations in the step-by-step solver.

Pro purchases

Monthly and yearly auto-renewable subscriptions and a one-time Lifetime purchase unlock the same AI tutor and scanner features. All original handbook, graph, solver, practice and visual-proof features remain free. App Store handles payments; we do not receive card details. Verified StoreKit entitlements are cached locally for offline access. Restore purchases uses your App Store account. Lifetime has no automatic renewal and does not cancel an existing subscription.

Optional update checks

The iOS app checks the public App Store listing for a newer compatible version. Apple receives a normal network request and IP address; questions, searches and practice results are not part of it. Check timestamps and the Later preference are stored locally. A failed check does not block the app.

Stores and the operating system

Google Play, the App Store and your operating system handle installation, update and crash data under their own rules. That happens outside the app, and we do not receive that data.

Deleting your settings

To delete what the app has saved, clear the app’s data or uninstall it. You can also reset Practice results in About. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription: manage it in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Pro purchases remain linked to your App Store account and can be restored. On iOS, local preferences may be included in the device backup that your system manages.

Children

The app is a study aid. It collects nothing from anyone, including children, and shows no advertising.

Contacting us

If you write to us by email, we receive your address and the contents of your message and use them only to reply. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

Policy changes

Any change to the app’s data practices will be reflected here with an updated date.
